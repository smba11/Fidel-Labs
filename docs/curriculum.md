# Fidel Labs V1 Curriculum

## Curriculum Principle

Teach fidel as a sound system, not as isolated symbols. Each lesson should connect symbol, sound, visual pattern, and usage in simple words.

V1 should avoid overwhelming learners with the full writing system at once. Teach one family at a time, then mix families often.

## Teaching Order

Start with high-utility, visually approachable families:

1. `ሀ` family: ሀ ሁ ሂ ሃ ሄ ህ ሆ
2. `ለ` family: ለ ሉ ሊ ላ ሌ ል ሎ
3. `መ` family: መ ሙ ሚ ማ ሜ ም ሞ
4. `ሰ` family: ሰ ሱ ሲ ሳ ሴ ስ ሶ
5. Mixed review: ሀ/ለ/መ/ሰ
6. `ረ` family: ረ ሩ ሪ ራ ሬ ር ሮ
7. `ሸ` family: ሸ ሹ ሺ ሻ ሼ ሽ ሾ
8. `ቀ` family: ቀ ቁ ቂ ቃ ቄ ቅ ቆ
9. First words using known fidel.

The full fidel system can be added in later units after the first learning loop is proven.

## Lesson Template

Each fidel lesson should follow this rhythm:

1. Introduce the family.
2. Let the learner tap each symbol and hear it.
3. Ask recognition questions.
4. Ask sound questions.
5. Mix similar symbols.
6. End with a short challenge.

## Exercise Mix

For a 10-question v1 lesson:

- 3 questions: choose fidel from sound.
- 3 questions: choose sound from fidel.
- 2 questions: match pairs.
- 2 questions: mixed review from previous lessons.

For word lessons:

- 2 questions: introduce word meaning.
- 3 questions: build word from fidel.
- 3 questions: choose word from audio.
- 2 questions: review component fidel.

## Romanization

Use a consistent beginner-friendly romanization in v1. It does not need to solve every linguistic edge case at first, but it must be stable in lessons.

Example:

- ሀ: ha
- ሁ: hu
- ሂ: hi
- ሃ: ha
- ሄ: he
- ህ: h
- ሆ: ho

Note: some fidel forms share similar romanized text in simplified beginner notation. The app should rely on audio and examples over romanization alone as content matures.

## First 10 Lessons

### Lesson 1: Welcome to Fidel

Goal: understand that fidel characters represent consonant-vowel syllables.

Exercises:

- Tap and hear examples.
- Match symbol to sound.
- Identify that fidel is read left-to-right.

### Lesson 2: The ሀ Family

Goal: recognize and hear the seven `ሀ` forms.

### Lesson 3: The ለ Family

Goal: recognize and hear the seven `ለ` forms.

### Lesson 4: ሀ + ለ Mix

Goal: distinguish first two families under mixed prompts.

### Lesson 5: The መ Family

Goal: recognize and hear the seven `መ` forms.

### Lesson 6: The ሰ Family

Goal: recognize and hear the seven `ሰ` forms.

### Lesson 7: First Mixed Review

Goal: strengthen ሀ, ለ, መ, and ሰ.

### Lesson 8: The ረ Family

Goal: recognize and hear the seven `ረ` forms.

### Lesson 9: The ሸ Family

Goal: distinguish `ሰ` and `ሸ`.

### Lesson 10: First Words

Goal: build simple words from known fidel.

Example content can include names, greetings, and short words, but every word should only use symbols already taught unless explicitly marked as preview content.
